How it works
  • Tap +1 after every point. Big red button, easy to find without looking.
  • Tap −1 to undo a wrong tap. Or hit "Undo last" for the most recent action.
  • Switch sets by tapping the Set 1 / 2 / 3 tab when teams move on.
  • Mark match complete when the match ends. Locks scores and updates standings.
  • No rules enforced. You enter what actually happened — site doesn't auto-end at 15.
